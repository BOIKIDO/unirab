## Core Definition
Lifetime Value (LTV) = gross profit collected over the lifespan of a customer.
Also called: Lifetime Gross Profit (LTGP), Customer Lifetime Value (CLV).

"If advertising is the machine that makes a business grow, LTV is the fuel."

## How to Calculate LTV

### Transactional/Product Business:
Gross Profit × Average Transactions Per Customer = LTGP
Example: $80 × 4 transactions = $360 LTGP

### Recurring Revenue Business:
Gross Profit / Churn % = LTGP
Example: $2,400 / 5% = $48,000 LTGP

Churn = customers who left / original amount
If 100 customers last month, 95 this month = 5/100 = 5% churn
(New signups during the period don't affect churn calculation)

## The Crazy 8 — 8 Ways to Increase LTV

### 1. RAISE PRICES
Most powerful lever. 20% price increase on 10% margin business = 3x profit (not 20%).
Test prices quarterly. Profitwell study: tight correlation between pricing test frequency and profitability.
Method: Raise 20% every 10 sales until dramatic drop, then go back to sweet spot.
Break-even formula: New conversion rate × New LTV must exceed old conversion rate × old LTV.

### 2. DECREASE COSTS (9 tactics)
a) Increase employee-to-customer ratio (5 clients/rep → 10 clients/rep = 50% cost cut)
b) Offshore talent (80%+ cost reduction)
c) Sell more similar customers to productize delivery (templates, automation)
d) Done-for-you → Done-with-you (increases ratio)
e) Cap usage (limit revisions, charge per use thereafter)
f) Lifetime → Annual (stop servicing indefinitely off one-time payment)
g) In-person → Remote
h) Cut meeting times (delete all recurring meetings quarterly, add back only what's needed)
i) Buy in bulk & prepay (10-20% guaranteed return via vendor discounts)

### 3. INCREASE # OF PURCHASES (3 ways)
a) Add Recurring — Membership, subscription, monthly accountability calls
b) Decrease Churn — From 10%/mo to 5%/mo doubles LTV. (Covered in Churn Checklist Playbook)
c) Follow Up — Quarterly reactivation campaigns. Provide value rest of time.

### 4. CROSS-SELL SOMETHING DIFFERENT
Sell complementary product. Add: conversion rate × gross profit of cross-sell to original LTV.
Example: $100 LTV + (20% × $100) = $120 New LTV.
Must not dramatically change who you serve or what you do. Easiest add-on with existing infrastructure.

### 5. SELL MORE (Increase Quantity — 3 ways)
a) Bulk — Prepay for year (12x)
b) More Often — Monthly → every 3 weeks (1.33x)
c) Bigger — 1 hour → 3 hours each time (3x)
Offer quantity upsell FIRST on sales calls, then downsell standard offer. See 20%+ lifts overnight.

### 6. SELL BETTER (Increase Quality)
Premium version with: faster response, better times/days, more locations, free cancellations, faster delivery, better service ratio, better communication method, better provider qualifications, live vs recorded, in-person vs remote, DIY/DWY/DFY, better expiration terms, more personalization.
Physical products: better ingredients, stronger/lighter/longer-lasting materials.
Offer premium first, then downsell standard. 20%+ lifts.

### 7. DOWNSELL FEWER (Lower Quantity)
Smaller amount, less frequent, smaller version. Only offer to UNQUALIFIED prospects.
Forbid sales team from selling qualified person a downsell. Prevents cannibalization.
Can make 50% more per visitor even with lower-priced sales.

### 8. DOWNSELL LOWER QUALITY
Reverse the quality upsell list. Slower response, less availability, fewer locations, fewer days, more limited hours, more junior employees, less convenient communication, more recorded, more remote, DFY→DWY→DIY, less personalization, worse guarantee.
Only offer to unqualified prospects.

## The Core Principle
"He who can make his customer more valuable to his business than to his competition, wins."
The cost of getting a customer can only hit zero. How much you make from each customer can go infinitely high.

## Money Model Framework (from script.txt)
A money model is a deliberate sequence of offers designed to maximize cash from customers.

### Three Stages:
1. **Get Cash** — Attraction offers get customers for less
2. **Get More Cash** — Upsell + Downsell offers make more from them faster
3. **Get the Most Cash** — Continuity offers maximize total spend

### Rule: Make enough profit to cover customer acquisition costs in 30 days or less.
Credit cards give 30 days of interest-free money. If you clear your balance within 30 days, you can recycle credit to acquire customers infinitely.

### Acceleration Math:
- 2x LTV + 2x customers + 2x speed = 8x growth
- 3x LTV + 3x customers + 3x speed = 27x growth
