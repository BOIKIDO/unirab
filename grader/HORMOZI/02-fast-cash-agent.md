# Fast Cash Agent — Knowledge Base
Source: $100M Fast Cash Playbook by Alex Hormozi (29 pages)

## Core Philosophy
"Revenue is vanity. Profit is sanity. Cash is reality."
Fast Cash Plays = limited time offers advertised to your WARMEST audiences (current customers, previous customers, engaged leads). Run once per quarter.

## The 10x The 10% Rule
Get 10% of your customers to pay 10x the price → you DOUBLE your revenue.
All extra revenue drops straight to the bottom line (minus delivery costs).
1 in 9 Americans is a millionaire — they can afford more if you provide the value.

## Fast Cash Play Economics
- Fast Cash Plays were ~15% of annual revenue but nearly HALF of profit
- Run at 90%+ gross margins
- Quarterly execution: Q1 $60K, Q2 $65K, Q3 $48K, Q4 $56K = $229K extra cash
- Result: 76% revenue increase, 4x net income, 22 new customers

## Offer Structure
- 1-3 core components + 3 bonuses
- Layer bonuses one at a time to increase price-to-value discrepancy
- All bonuses available to all buyers regardless of when they buy

## Pricing
- 10x to 50x your current average cart value
- Cap buyers at 5-10% of customer base
- Sell out fast → more compelling next time

## Push to Consult Sequence (7-Day Campaign)
| Day | Action |
|---|---|
| 7 days out | Teaser text: "something big is coming" |
| 6 days out | Announcement email with core offer + CTA to schedule consult |
| 5 days out | Recap + first bonus |
| 3 days out | Sales update: how many sold, how many left |
| 2 days out | X spots left + second bonus |
| 24 hours out (AM) | Warning + best bonus (worth more than price tag) |
| 12 hours out (PM) | Sold out announcement |
| Optional | Back out one spot left |

## Push to Automated Checkout Sequence
| Timing | Action |
|---|---|
| 5 days out | Primer text |
| 4 days out | Announcement email |
| 2 days out | Cart opens in 48 hours |
| 24 hours out | First bonus + set alarm reminder |
| 60 min out | Payment method ready reminder |
| Cart open | Best bonus (immediate benefit) |
| 60 min post | Sales update |
| 6 hours post | X spots left + unmentioned bonus |
| 12 hours post | Sold out |
| Next day | Back out one spot |

## Bonus Types for Sequences
- Status Bonus (better parking, VIP access)
- Secret Bonus (special training, trade secrets)
- Access Bonus (in-person retreat, luxury experience)

## Three Offer Examples

### B2C Fast Cash Offer — Fitness ($10,000)
- Core: Tailored supplements, meals, workouts, photoshoot
- Bonus 1: Year-long nutrition plan + meal prep intro
- Bonus 2: 1-on-1 workouts 3x/week
- Bonus 3: 24/7 personal cell phone access

### B2B Fast Cash Offer — Marketing Agency ($50,000/year)
- Core: Script, film, edit, post all content 4x/year
- Bonus 1: Additional social media platform
- Bonus 2: Lead magnet creation
- Bonus 3: Organic funnel with video sales letters

### Recurring Max Cash Offer (Monthly × 12)
- Offer: 1 Year to [ULTIMATE RESULT]
- Bonus 1: Custom work on struggle area
- Bonus 2: Speed enhancer
- Bonus 3: Virtual guarantee

## 5 Reasons to Run Every 90 Days
1. Cleans up pipeline of qualified-but-on-the-fence leads
2. Increases value of every customer
3. Shows something new and interesting → keeps engagement
4. Allows time to deliver and reset between plays
5. Additional revenue drops straight to bottom line

## Key Sales Email Formulas
- "I want to help you [goal] faster. And I want to make it easier. And I'm gonna make it cheaper."
- Create a reason for the deal: "I want to invest in [big thing] which will benefit you in these ways..."
- "Expires Saturday at midnight PST"
- "What if there is no NEXT ONE?"
- "41.7% of people will spend more on their cell phones than on their goals"
- PS: "ONE MORE THING I'm giving away to the [X] customers..."
