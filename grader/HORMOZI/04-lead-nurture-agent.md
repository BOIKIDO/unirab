# Lead Nurture & Sales Agent — Knowledge Base
Source: $100M Lead Nurture Playbook by Alex Hormozi (45 pages)

## Core Definitions
- **Schedule Rate** = % of engaged leads who schedule an appointment
- **Show Rate** = % of scheduled leads who show up to the appointment
- **Throughput** = total % of engaged leads who show up

Example: 100 leads, 50% schedule rate = 50 scheduled. 50% show rate = 25 shown. Throughput = 25%.

"If they do not show, they cannot buy."

## The Four Pillars of Lead Nurture

### Pillar I: Availability
The biggest lever on getting leads to show. More time slots = more schedules = more shows = more purchases.

**Tactics:**
1. **Take appointments more days per week** — 7 days/week = 40% more available. "You pay rent 7 days/week. Might as well make money 7 days/week."
2. **Take appointments more hours per day** — 6am-6pm PST covers highest percentage of US sales
3. **More flexible appointment times** — 4 options per hour (every 15 min) instead of 1-2
4. **Inbound + Outbound + Self-Scheduling** — Do all three simultaneously

**Self-Scheduler Optimization:**
- Header confirms they're in right place
- Make available dates/times super obvious immediately
- Eliminate as many steps as possible
- Don't make leads re-enter info they already provided

**When Too Many Bad Appointments:** Add friction — require watching a video, reading a sales letter, showing price before scheduler appears. Decreases appointments, increases quality.

### Pillar II: Speed
Money loves speed. Wealth loves time. Poverty loves indecision.

**Three Speed Levers:**
1. **Speed to First Contact** — Contact within 60 seconds = 391% increase in conversions (Velocify). Companies contacting within 1 hour are 7x more likely to qualify (Harvard Business Review). 78% buy from company that responds first (Lead Connect).
2. **Speed to First Appointment** — Max 3 days out. Same-day appointments have highest show rates. "I'm talking to you right now" = 100% show rate.
3. **Speed of Response** — Respond to messages immediately between schedule and appointment.

**Speed Litmus Test:** If you aren't getting "Man that was fast! You guys are on top of it!" at least once a day, you're not calling fast enough.

**5 Outcomes When Calling to Schedule:**
1. Doesn't respond → remove from calendar (or double-book if in-person)
2. Contacted + unqualified → remove, give free useful stuff or references
3. Contacted + qualified + free right now → BEST scenario (guaranteed show, go for close or hot handoff)
4. Contacted + qualified + pulled appointment forward
5. Contacted + qualified + appointment confirmed

**Pull-Forward Script:**
"Hey, this is [NAME] calling from [COMPANY] on a recorded line… I saw you booked a time for [X]. Just calling to confirm some details. Is now a terrible time? [QUALIFY: who they are, what they want, what they've tried]. Great news: I think we can help. Better news: I have an opening that canceled later today at 4pm… unless now is a good time?"

**Hot Handoff Script:**
"You're in luck. You're going to speak with one of our top experts on X. He's helped Y [people] achieve [result]. We're fortunate to have him. Let me check his calendar... I'm gonna slide you in so you can get this taken care of ASAP. The moment we get off, I'll introduce you via text. Does [NUMBER] work?"

### Pillar III: Personalization
6 ways to personalize:

1. **Use Lead's Preferred Communication Method** — Contact everywhere (call, text, DM, email), then continue where they respond. "Reach out like you actually mean it — not just going through the motions."

2. **Qualify Leads** — Use applications before sales calls. Remove unqualified leads from calendar to free time for good ones.

3. **Best Leads to Best Closers** — Score leads 1-5 or Red-Yellow-Green. Route best leads to best closers. (This took a timeshare company from $200M to $1B.)

4. **Segment Messaging** — Easy: 5 min research before every call. Hard: Different email/text sequences per lead category (revenue level, weight loss goal, etc.). HubSpot improved email ROI 7x by segmenting.

5. **Incentivize Showing Up:**
   - **Push Incentive** (Gift Card): Give gift before appointment, no strings attached. Reciprocity drives shows. "Hey! I just sent you a $5 gift card so you're caffeinated for our meeting."
   - **Pull Incentive** (A/B): Leads choose between two options that both assume showing up. "Do you prefer black or pink?" Once they pick, they show almost every time.

6. **Proof** — Match testimonials to lead demographics. Weave proof between schedule and show. If they ghost, send results of people who signed up at the same time (makes them feel left behind).

**A/B Incentive Examples:** Quantity, start dates, type, payment preference, flavors, time slots, media, delivery speeds, sizes, colors, materials, personnel, communication method.

**Pro Tips:**
- Get team iPhones for blue-bubble texting (higher response rates)
- Local area code call wrapping (higher pick-up rates)

### Pillar IV: Volume
"Volume negates luck."
44% of salespeople stop after first attempt. Average: 1.3 attempts. Don't be average.

**Scheduling Appointment Cadence:**
1. Call within 5 min of opt-in
2. Double-dial immediately (many phones block first call, let second through)
3. Leave voicemail
4. Send text immediately after voicemail
5. Double-dial + text 2 more times that day (few hours between)
6. Call 2x for next 2 days (early + late, text after second call each day)
7. Call + text 1x/day for next 4 days
8. After week 1 → transition to long-term nurture (free value + soft CTAs)

**Automated Reminders:**
- Immediately: confirmation of time, date, number, person meeting with
- 24 hours before
- 12 hours out
- 3 hours from appointment
- Include area code (2x pick-up rates)
- Use lead's local time zone

**Manual Reminders (from real phone):**
- Night before: "Hey [NAME], looking forward to chatting tomorrow at [TIME]. Just confirming you're still good."
- Morning of: "See you at [TIME]! Here's the number I'll call from: [NUMBER]"
- 60 min prior: "One hour! Excited to chat."

**BAMFAM — Book A Meeting From A Meeting:**
NEVER get off the call with "We'll circle back later." Pull up the calendar right then and there. If you can't schedule with them in real-time, you certainly won't after putting them in limbo.

**Manual Reminder Structure (ACA Method):**
1. Acknowledge ("Great hearing from you!")
2. Compliment ("Love that you're thinking ahead about X")
3. Ask the next question ("I've actually got an opening today at 4 if you're available. If not, no worries.")

## Execution & Culture
- "Volume negates luck"
- "Yellow is the new gold" — less-qualified leads are opportunities for practice and sometimes surprise you
- "Be a garbage man" — take all the leads others reject
- "Every sale is an opportunity for free practice"
- Track 3 metrics per sales rep: Show rate, Close rate, Call-to-close %
- Show rate = work ethic. Close rate = skill.
- The best closers are typically the best setters — great entry path for new reps
- Commission bump for show-up rates: 5-30% of what salesperson makes on a sale
- Non-monetary: attention, affection, approval for high show rates

## Full Throughput Optimization Summary
Convenience (availability) → Speed (contact fast) → Personalization (make it for them) → Volume (reach out many times) → Execute (build culture around process)
