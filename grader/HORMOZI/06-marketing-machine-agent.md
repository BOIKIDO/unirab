# Marketing Machine Agent — Knowledge Base
Source: $100M Marketing Machine by Alex Hormozi (30 pages)

## Core Problem
A business that depends on the founder's face is worth LESS and is UNSALEABLE.
Solution: Build a system that generates ads WITHOUT the founder.

## The "Hit By a Bus" Question
"What would keep the business growing tomorrow if I got hit by a bus today?"
Two-part answer:
1. List EVERYTHING you do in the business
2. Build systems to do those things without you

## The Breakthrough Insight
"The bottom of the list feeds the top of the list."
Sales calls, onboarding, support calls, and upsell calls all generate advertising proof.
Every customer interaction IS a potential ad.

## Key Statistic
80% (40 out of 50) of Gym Launch's top-performing ads did NOT have Alex's face — they were customer-generated.

## The 9 Nodes of the Marketing Machine

### 1. LIFECYCLE ADS
Capture the full customer journey through recorded calls: Before (sales calls) → During (support calls) → After (ascension/milestone calls).

Checklist:
- Add compliance/permission language to all customer contracts
- Record ALL calls: sales, onboarding, support, ascension, milestone
- Buy extra storage, turn off auto-delete
- Create calendar events for all calls with client name in title
- Sales: send happy-customer-calls to marketing weekly
- Marketing: cross-reference calendar events for accuracy
- Clip: before moment (sales call), during moment (1-2 support calls), after moment (upsell/testimonial call)
- Create friendly labeling system for clips
- Run ads and print money

**Local Business variant — "Upon Delivery" Ads:** Capture raw reaction moments (kitchen reveal, scale moment, veneers reveal, "you got the job!"). These become the hook (first 3-5 seconds of ad).

### 2. SOCIAL MEDIA SCRAPE
- Create hashtag for community (#gymlord, #poweredbyprestige)
- Set regular reminders to grab tagged media across all platforms
- Save Instagram/Snapchat/Facebook stories before they disappear (24hr window)
- Tag/save with relevant facts and searchable names
- Separate studs from duds: create separate folder for outperforming organic media
- Good organic performance correlates highly with good ad/outbound performance
- Use best-performing images/videos with already-performing ad copy

### 3. IN-PERSON EVENTS
Three ad types to capture:

**Stage Ads:**
- Get picture on stage with people celebrating behind (visual proof of size/scope)
- Multiple selfies + video
- Do this end of Day 1 or before lunch Day 2

**Professional Testimonials:**
- Reserve 2 separate rooms (event room + recording room, or 2-3 extra hotel rooms)
- Hire at least one extra camera team (2 men on cameras + 1 runner)
- MUST TEST lighting, sound, multiple mics before event
- Pick customers for testimonials ahead of time, confirm in writing
- Print hit list on clipboard, check names off
- Announce at event beginning, introduce handler
- Incentivize: customers get bonus for giving testimonial; handler gets $50 for every extra person captured
- Plan timing: avoid keynotes. Grab during mornings, lunches, breaks, end of day

**6-Point Testimonial Script:**
1. (Internal Struggle) What was rock bottom before? What did you hate?
2. (External Struggle) What were your objective metrics?
3. (Skepticism) What were your main concerns about buying/joining?
4. (Overcome) What made you do it anyway?
5. (External Victory) What are your objective metrics now?
6. (Internal Victory) What's been the best moment since? What can you do now you couldn't before?

- Most important part: THE HOOK. Have them say a few different hooks before telling their story.
- HAVE THEM SAY THEIR NAME at the beginning for matching.

**Man in the Street:**
- Second two-man camera team
- Prepare 10 core questions ahead of time
- Get 10+ responses per question
- Turn into fast-hitting highlight reels
- Questions: "How many new customers since you started?" "What were you making before vs now?" "How much weight have you lost?" "What was your old credit score vs now?"
- Can also use 6-point script in rapid-fire (under 2 min per person)

### 4. COMMUNICATION & COMMUNITIES SCRAPE
Three sources:
- Support/Account Rep Chats
- Posts and comments in managed communities
- Reviews & comments scrape (Glassdoor, Yelp, Google, Facebook, TrustPilot, Amazon)

Checklist:
- Create list of key win-indicating terms (PIF, Sales, $, Profit, Revenue, Monthly, close, closed, leads, show, scheduled, win)
- Shared folder for all support reps
- Weekly reminder to search key terms in messages → screenshot → add to folder
- Reward people who submit screenshots (kudos, $100 gift card for winning ads)
- Community: Create "wins" post category, encourage Friday wins posts
- Screen record scrolling through community wins for video ads

### 5. BONUSES & AWARDS
**Bonus Unlocks:** Create a sexy, high-value thing. Attach a price to it. Tell customers they can buy it OR unlock it for free by submitting a testimonial video hitting the 6 points.

**Award Testimonials:** Create an aspirational milestone, associate it with status. 4 forms of proof from ONE award:
1. Written post showing actual numbers (Proof 1)
2. Video testimonial sent to email (Proof 2)
3. Unboxing video of the trophy/trinket (Proof 3)
4. On-stage/in-person award presentation recording (Proof 4)
Make the trophy big, cool, and expensive. It'll make more back.

### 6. COMPETITIONS
Run 1-2x/year max.
- Set announcement date + submission deadline
- Crazy award for winner (6 months free service/product)
- Give results template + 6-point testimonial script + last winner example
- Announce via all internal channels
- Countdown to deadline
- Personal invitations + regular follow-up with top 50 target customers
- Cut off submissions, run all as ads, set 30-day reminder to count conversions
- Winner = most conversions as an ad over 30 days

### 7. DELIVERY
Customer reaction videos: unboxing, first-time user, long-time user, customer demonstrations.

## Weekly Marketing Machine Output
- List of names of people who got great results
- Scraped social media (images + videos)
- Chat/messaging screenshots from account reps
- Community post/comment testimonials
- New 5-star reviews on external sites
- Bonus unlocks (post screenshots + videos)
- Award deliveries + customer recognition
- Customer reaction videos

## Implementation Order (Start Here)
1. Competition (big influx)
2. Chat/messaging scrape (already have this, minimal editing)
3. Community scrape
4. Reviews scrape
5. Social media scrape
6. Bonus unlocks (requires creating something to give away)
7. Award unlocks (requires creating something + status)
8. Events (lot of work but pays for itself in proof alone)
9. Lifecycle (most editing horsepower, very unique, time-consuming)

## Core Principle
"One > None" — If starting out, one or two processes can completely change your life and marketing. Start with the easiest one. Come back and add more.

## Customer is the Star
"It's never really been about you… it's about them. They are the star of the story. So — put them in the spotlight, not you."
