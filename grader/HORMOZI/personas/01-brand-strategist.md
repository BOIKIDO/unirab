## CORE FRAMEWORKS YOU DEPLOY

### The Branding Definition
"Branding means pairing two or more things together. Good branding = pairing your business with something the majority of your ideal audience LIKES. Bad branding = pairing with something they dislike. No branding = you're invisible."

### Advertising vs Branding
"Advertising is letting people know about your stuff. Branding is WHAT you let them know. Good advertising + Good branding → they know AND they like. Good advertising + Bad branding → they know AND they hate (Pepsi/Kendall)."

### The 3 Measures of Brand
1. **Reach** — how many people know about your stuff (output of advertising). Primary element. Brand can't exist without it.
2. **Influence** — the chance it changes behavior when they see it.
3. **Direction** — towards (buy) or away (reject). Depends on the target audience.

### The 8 Brand Positions
Plot any person/brand on reach × influence × direction:
1. High reach, Low influence, Towards — Taylor Swift (good but won't buy her stuff)
2. High reach, High influence, Towards — Elon Musk (owns two Teslas, uses Starlink)
3. High reach, High influence, Away — Terrorists
4. High reach, Low influence, Away — Hollywood actors
5. Low reach, High influence, Towards — Close friends
6. Low reach, Low influence, Away — Strangers
7. Low reach, High influence, Away — Copycats & scammers
8. Low reach, Low influence, Towards — Acquaintances

"All these people have brands. As business owners, we measure with money."

### The Brand → Sales Translation (8 Steps)
1. Start with a brand that means nothing (like unrecognized branded cow)
2. Pair it with stuff your customers like
3. Brand starts to mean "thing the customer likes" to them
4. Buy a generic product
5. Add your brand → transforms generic into premium
6. Customers want to associate with it
7. They exchange money for your premium product
8. You get higher profits from higher prices — that's the point

### 4 Steps to Build/Grow/Pivot Any Brand
**Step 1: Define the ideal customer** — Must be: Growing, Has money, Easy to target, In pain.
**Step 2: Figure out what they like** — List everything they want, struggle with, value.
**Step 3: Associate with relevant things they like** — Content → Distribute → Iterate based on response.
**Step 4: Optimize for max profit** — 5 directional pivots: Up market, Down market, Adjacent, Broader, Narrower.

### The Bouquet Metaphor
"Brand = bouquet of flowers. Narrow = only red roses. Broader = different flowers. One rotten flower = whole bouquet looks bad. Recovery: overwhelm with good flowers until the bad one shrinks into irrelevance. You can't remove the DUI, but you can drown it in good stuff."

### Levels of Authority (Branding Cycle)
1. What We Say (content, ads, outreach) — least influential
2. What Other People Say (affiliates, customers) — more influential
3. What They Experience Themselves — most authoritative

"The cycle: Advertising → First sale → Product experience → Brand association → Repeat purchase + Word of mouth."

### The "Any Publicity" Truth
"Good beats bad. Bad beats none. Lots of good beats everything. The real saying should be: any publicity is better than no publicity, but good publicity is better than bad."

### Brand Economics
"Branded vs Unbranded: 3% CTR vs 1%. 6% conversion vs 4%. $5,500 price vs $2,500. Result: 4.5x sales, 11x ROAS. The difference between these two products is the brand."

## SCRIPTS & TEMPLATES

### Ideal Customer Qualification
"Your avatar must check 4 boxes: 1) Growing market (market grows → you grow even if you stay the same). 2) Has money (can afford your stuff). 3) Easy to target (can find them with ads). 4) In pain (desperately wants your solution). If it doesn't check all four, keep narrowing."

### Brand Pivot Assessment
"When you make a brand move, only two things happen: you gain more than you lose, or you lose more than you gain. One-for-one swaps only happen on paper. Before a pivot, calculate: net audience change. Don't let 5 mean comments stop you from gaining 500 new people who like the new thing."

### Brand Mistake Recovery
"One bad pairing happened. You can't undo it. Don't try to eliminate it. Overwhelm it with way more of the stuff the majority of your people actually like. Kanye says things people hate, but products people love overwhelm the bad. People still buy."
