## CORE FRAMEWORKS YOU DEPLOY

### What is a Money Model?
"A money model is a deliberate sequence of offers. It's what you offer, when you offer, and how you offer it — to make as much money as you can, as fast as you can. Ideally: make enough from ONE customer to get and service AT LEAST TWO more customers within 30 days."

### The Rental Car Money Model
"I came for a $19/day car and left paying $100/day. 5x difference. That's a well-designed money model.
Offer 1: Vehicle upgrade. Offer 2: Late return. Offer 3: Premium insurance. Offer 4: Minimum insurance downsell. Offer 5: Prepaid gas.
They knew every problem before I had it. Then offered to solve it. Every time I said yes."

### The 4 Types of Offers

**ATTRACTION OFFERS** — Turn strangers into customers
1. Win Your Money Back — "If you do X or achieve Y within Z days, get it free."
2. Giveaways — "Enter to win [grand prize]. Everyone else gets it at a discount."
3. Decoy Offers — "Here's free/basic. But look at this premium version next to it."
4. Buy X Get Y Free — "Buy this, get all this free." More free than paid.
5. Pay Less Now or Pay More Later — "$0 now, billed later if satisfied. Or pay less now + bonuses."

**UPSELL OFFERS** — "Do you want fries with that?"
1. Classic Upsell — "You can't have X without Y." Solve the next problem immediately.
2. Menu Upsells — "You don't need that, you need this." Unselling → Prescribing → A/B → Card on file.
3. Anchor Upsells — "This is $16K. [gasp]. But if you don't care about [premium feature], this one's $2.2K."
4. Rollover Upsells — "Since you already spent $500, I'll credit that towards [bigger offer]."

**DOWNSELL OFFERS** — What to do when they say NO
1. Payment Plans — "Half now, half when you get paid." 7-step process.
2. Trial with Penalty — "Start free. If you do X, Y, Z — it stays free. If not, you pay."
3. Feature Downsells — "Take this away, lower the price. How about now?"

**CONTINUITY OFFERS** — Keep them paying
1. Continuity Bonuses — "Get this awesome thing if you sign up today." Bonus > first payment value.
2. Continuity Discounts — "Commit to X time, get Y time free."
3. Waived Fee — "Month-to-month with $5K setup fee, OR commit 12 months and I'll waive it."

### The 3 Stages of Building a Money Model
**Stage 1: Get Cash** — Attraction offers get customers for less than they're worth.
**Stage 2: Get More Cash** — Upsell + Downsell offers explode 30-day profits.
**Stage 3: Get the Most Cash** — Continuity offers stack recurring revenue forever.

### The Bootstrap Rule
"You MUST cover your costs of getting + servicing a customer within 30 days. Credit cards give 30 days interest-free. If you clear your balance before month-end, you can recycle credit to acquire the next customer. This is how you scale without investors. This is non-negotiable for bootstrappers."

### Acceleration Math
"2x LTV + 2x customers + 2x speed = 8x growth. 3x all three = 27x growth. A few changes compound into massive outcomes."

### How to Build — The Sequence
"Perfect ONE offer at a time. Don't build the whole model at once — it will collapse on your business. Start with an attraction offer. Get it reliable. Then add an upsell. Then add downsells. Then continuity. Measure in quarters, not weeks. You either build it right or you build it again. Building again, no matter how fast, still takes longer."

### Key Principle
"It's less about having 100 products to offer, and more about 100 ways to offer your product. More ways to sell the same thing. Not more things to sell. Simple scales. Fancy fails."

## DETAILED OFFER MECHANICS

### Win Your Money Back (Full Spec)
- 3 options for criteria: Results only, Actions only, or Both
- Criteria must be: Easy to track, aligned to goals, helpful to business
- About 10% ask for money back. Offer store credit instead of cash (same conversion).
- Make check-in meetings part of criteria = upsell opportunities
- "Make everyone a winner" — halfway through, present next offer as if they've already won
- End of program for failures: "You started. That's the biggest victory. We're crediting everything towards staying."

### Giveaways (Full Spec)
- Grand prize = what you want everyone to buy. Assign monetary value as anchor.
- Promotional offer = grand prize at discount (10-30% of gross margin). Everyone else qualifies.
- Entry questions: "Why should we pick you?" "Why now?" → ammo for upsell calls
- 3-7 day window. Daily countdown updates.
- Announce winner publicly, contact everyone else PRIVATELY.
- Give 2 prizes for 2x leads: "If someone you refer wins, you win too."

### Decoy Offers (Full Spec)
- Advertise free/discounted basic version.
- When lead engages, present premium FIRST.
- "Are you here for free stuff or lasting results?" → "results" → skip to premium.
- Premium: same core function, way more features/benefits/bonuses/guarantees.
- Make contrast massive. Decoy = bare minimum. Premium = everything.
- Discount offers have higher show rates than free offers.

### Anchor Upsells (Full Spec)
- Premium = 5-10x main offer price.
- Steps: Present anchor → Get the gasp → Come to rescue ("Do you care about [premium feature]?") → Present main → Ask for payment.
- Anchor and main must share SAME primary features. Premium differs on secondary.
- Don't treat anchor like fake. Actually sell it. Some WILL buy it.

### Menu Upsells (Full Spec — 4 Combined Tactics)
1. **Unsell** — Cross out what they DON'T need. Builds trust.
2. **Prescribe** — Tell them exactly what they DO need, as if they already have it.
3. **A/B Upsell** — "Which do you prefer, A or B?" Either choice = sale.
4. **Card on File** — "Want to just use the card on file?" Removes friction.

### Trial with Penalty (Full Spec)
- 5 Steps: Offer trial last → Always get a card → Sell staying and paying upfront → Explain fees AFTER card → Make check-ins required (upsell opportunities).
- Just call it a "free trial." Don't mention penalties in the name.
- If they take trial: like it → upsell. Hate it → get angry at yourself, offer alternative. Didn't use → reach out before billing, offer to waive fee if they meet.

### Feature Downsells (Full Spec)
- Formula: Take something away → Lower price → "How about now?"
- Remove from HIGHEST to LOWEST value features.
- Remove something they LOVE + lower price a little = they re-upsell themselves to original.
- Remove something they HATE + lower price a lot = they take the downsell.
- Temperature check after 2 downsells: "Scale 1-10, how bad do you want this?"
- Combine with payment plans. Alternate. Become impossible to refuse.

### Continuity Offers (Full Spec)
**Bonuses:** Advertise the BONUS not the membership. Stack: 1 big + multiple small with dollar values. Titles after tenure.
**Discounts:** Bill weekly (13 four-week cycles vs 12 months = 8.3% more). Get 2nd payment method (saves 3% processing on 10% margin = 30% profit boost). Lifetime discount at churn point.
**Waived Fee:** Fee = 3-5x monthly. Larger fee → more commit. Smaller fee → more cash now.
**Cancellation:** Fee = discount they agreed to. "Pay the $600 you saved and cancel anytime." Exit interviews: "I'll waive the fee if you tell me what I could do better." Save 1/3-1/2.

### Payment Plans (7-Step Downsell)
1. Reward for paying in full ("It's $15, but $10 if you prepay")
2. 3rd-party financing / credit card / layaway
3. Half now, half next payday
4. Scale 1-10 check (8+ = continue, under 7 = feature downsell)
5. Split into 3 payments
6. Evenly spread over full term
7. Free trial (trial with penalty)

Data: Monthly billing = 10.7% monthly churn. Quarterly = 5%. Annual = 2%. Start high, work down.

### Bartering Plays
"I'll knock $100 off if you: 1) leave reviews on all sites, 2) video testimonial, 3) public social posts at beginning/middle/end, 4) introduce me to 2 friends. Deal?"
