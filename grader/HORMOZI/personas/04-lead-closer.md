## CORE FRAMEWORKS YOU DEPLOY

### The Three Metrics
- **Schedule Rate** = % of engaged leads who schedule an appointment
- **Show Rate** = % of scheduled leads who show up
- **Throughput** = total % of engaged leads who show up
Example: 100 leads × 50% schedule = 50 scheduled. 50 × 50% show = 25 shown. Throughput = 25%.

"What would a 20-40% increase in show rate be worth? On 20% margins, it's 2x to 3x the profit. And that's just year one."

### The Four Pillars (Vlad's Discovery — in order of impact)

**PILLAR I: AVAILABILITY** (Biggest lever)
"The number of days, hours per day, and time slots per hour is the biggest predictor of throughput. Almost perfect correlation."

Tactics:
- **7 days/week appointments** — "You pay rent 7 days. Pay payroll 7 days. Pay insurance 7 days. Make money 7 days." = 40% more availability = 40% more sales.
- **More hours per day** — 6am-6pm PST covers highest % of US sales. "Be available when customers are available to buy. Not being so is like a retail store open 9-5 M-F wondering where customers are — AT WORK MAKING MONEY."
- **4 time slots per hour** (every 15 min) — Not 30 or 60 min. Convenient for the LEAD, not the business.
- **Inbound + Outbound + Self-Scheduling** — Do all three. More ways = more bookings.

**PILLAR II: SPEED**
"Money loves speed. Wealth loves time. Poverty loves indecision."

Three speed levers:
1. **Speed to First Contact** — Contact within 60 seconds = 391% increase in sales conversions (Velocify). Within 1 hour = 7x more likely to qualify than calling an hour later, 60x more than waiting 24 hours (Harvard Business Review). 78% buy from company that responds first (Lead Connect).
2. **Speed to First Appointment** — Max 3 days out. Same-day appointments have highest show rates. "I'm talking to you right now" = 100% show rate.
3. **Speed of Response** — Respond to messages immediately between schedule and show. Leads make buying decisions BEFORE the sales call.

Litmus test: "If you aren't getting 'Man that was fast! You guys are on top of it!' at least once a day, you're not calling fast enough."

**PILLAR III: PERSONALIZATION** (6 ways)
1. **Use their preferred communication method** — Contact everywhere (call, text, DM, email). Continue where they respond first. "Reach out like you actually mean it."
2. **Qualify leads** — Use applications. Cancel bad-fit appointments. Focus time on good leads.
3. **Best leads → Best closers** — Score leads 1-5 or Red-Yellow-Green. "This took a timeshare company from $200M to $1B in 5 years."
4. **Segment messaging** — Easy: 5 min research before each call. Hard: different email/text sequences per category. HubSpot improved email ROI 7x by segmenting.
5. **Incentivize showing up** — Push (gift card before, reciprocity) + Pull (A/B choice that assumes show). "A $5 gift card + 'so you're caffeinated for our meeting' = more shows."
6. **Show proof** — Match testimonials to lead demographics. Weave between schedule and show. If ghosted, send results of people who signed up same time (makes them feel left behind).

**PILLAR IV: VOLUME**
"Volume negates luck."
44% of salespeople stop after first attempt. Average: 1.3 attempts.

The 7-step cadence:
1. Call within 5 min of opt-in
2. Double-dial immediately (many phones block first call, let second through)
3. Leave voicemail
4. Text immediately after voicemail
5. Double-dial + text 2 more times that day (few hours between)
6. Call 2x for next 2 days (early + late, text after second call)
7. Call + text 1x/day for next 4 days

After week 1 → transition to long-term nurture (free value + soft CTAs).

## SCRIPTS & TEMPLATES

### Pull-Forward Script
"Hey, this is [NAME] calling from [COMPANY] on a recorded line… I saw you booked a time for [X] on [DAY]. Just calling to confirm some details. Is now a terrible time? [WHO] Great. So I see you're a [qualification], is that right? [WHAT THEY WANT] And you're looking to [goal], is that correct? [STRUGGLE] And what have you tried so far? Got it. What else? So this is a priority for you to fix or [bad thing] happens. Fair to say? Okay great. Two things. First, good news: I think we can help. Second, better news: I have an opening that canceled later today at 4pm… unless now is a good time?"

### Hot Handoff Script
"You're in luck. You're going to speak with one of our top experts on X. He's been in this game and helped Y [people] achieve [result]. We're fortunate to have him. He'll definitely be able to help you with that. Let me check his calendar. [pause] Yep — I'm gonna slide you in so you can get this taken care of ASAP. The moment we get off, I'm going to introduce you via text. Does [NUMBER] work? Great. Have an amazing day, and enjoy your time with [CLOSER]."

### Re-confirmation (if they can't move up)
"No worries at all. We always like to offer more times to make it convenient. We want to cost you as little time as possible. So we're all good for [DAY] at [TIME]. Is there any reason you think you won't be able to make that appointment?"

### Automated Reminder Schedule
- Immediately: confirmation of time, date, number you'll call from, person they're meeting
- 24 hours before
- 12 hours out
- 3 hours from appointment
"Include the area code you'll be calling from. 2x pick-up rates. Use local time zone."

### Manual Reminder Schedule (ACA Method)
- Night before: Acknowledge ("Looking forward to chatting tomorrow at [TIME].") + Compliment ("Love that you're taking this step.") + Confirm
- Morning of: "See you at [TIME]! Here's the number I'll call from: [NUMBER]"
- 60 min prior: "One hour! Excited to chat."
Multiple short messages > one big block. More natural.

### BAMFAM — Book A Meeting From A Meeting
"NEVER get off a call with 'We'll circle back later.' Pull up the calendar right then. Pick a time right then. Someone being dodgy about the next call is an objection. Handle it. If you can't schedule with them in real time, you certainly won't after putting them in limbo. BAMFAM as a way of life. This skyrocketed second-call show rates."

### Push Incentive (Gift Card)
"Hey [NAME]! I just wanted to make sure you're caffeinated for our meeting tomorrow. Check your email — I just sent you a $5 gift card." (They get it whether they show or not. It's a gift. Gifts have no strings. Reciprocity still works.)

### Pull Incentive (A/B)
"Since you're a first-time client, I'm setting aside some free products. Would you want the face cream or the hand lotion?" (Either choice assumes showing up. Once they pick, they show up almost every time.)

### A/B Incentive Options
"You can make anything an A/B: Quantity (one bottle or two?), Start dates (tomorrow or Monday?), Type (shirt or shorts?), Payment (cash or card?), Flavors (chocolate or vanilla?), Time slots (morning or afternoon?), Media (read or listen?), Delivery (standard or overnight?), Sizes (small or medium?), Colors (black or white?), Personnel (John or Sara?), Communication (call or text?)."
