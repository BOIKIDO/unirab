### The 9 Nodes of the Marketing Machine

**1. LIFECYCLE ADS** — Capture the full journey
Before moment (sales call) → During moment (support calls) → After moment (upsell/milestone call).
Implementation: legal compliance in contracts → record ALL calls → extra storage → calendar events with client names → sales sends happy calls to marketing weekly → cross-reference → clip the three moments → label → run.

**2. SOCIAL MEDIA SCRAPE** — Grab what's already being posted
- Create community hashtags (#gymlord, #poweredbyprestige, #neverskipdessert)
- Set reminders to grab tagged media across all platforms weekly
- Save stories before 24hr expiry
- Separate studs from duds — organic performance correlates with ad performance

**3. IN-PERSON EVENTS** — 3 ad types
- Stage ads: pictures on stage with crowd behind, selfies, video. End of day 1.
- Professional testimonials: separate room, 2-man camera team + runner, test equipment, pick customers ahead, print hit list, 6-point script.
- Man in the street: second camera team, 10 prepared questions, 10+ responses each → highlight reels.

**4. COMMUNICATION & COMMUNITIES SCRAPE**
- Support/account rep chats: list of win-indicating keywords (PIF, Sales, $, Profit, Revenue, Monthly, close, closed, leads, show, scheduled, win), weekly search, screenshot, folder.
- Community posts: "wins" category, Friday wins posts, Monday scrape, screen record scrolling.
- Review sites: Glassdoor, Yelp, Google, Facebook, TrustPilot, Amazon — sort by newest, screenshot.

**5. BONUSES & AWARDS**
- Bonus unlocks: Create sexy high-value thing → attach price → offer free in exchange for testimonial video hitting 6 points.
- Award testimonials: Create aspirational milestone + status symbol (sword & shield for Gym Lords). Send engraved one. Present generic one on stage. = 4 forms of proof from 1 award (written post, testimonial video, unboxing video, stage presentation).

**6. COMPETITIONS**
Run 1-2x/year. Raw iPhone testimonial competition. Winner = most conversions as an ad over 30 days. Prize: 6 months free. Send 6-point script + last year's winner example. Personal invitations to top 50 target customers.

**7. DELIVERY** — Reaction videos
Unboxing, first-time user, long-time user, customer demonstrations.

### The 6-Point Testimonial Script (MEMORIZED)
1. **(Internal Struggle)** What was rock bottom before doing this? What did you have to do that you hated? What things did you want that you couldn't do?
2. **(External Struggle)** What were your objective metrics? Numbers, stats.
3. **(Skepticism)** What were your main concerns about buying/clicking/joining?
4. **(Overcome)** What made you do it anyway?
5. **(External Victory)** What are your objective metrics now? Numbers, stats.
6. **(Internal Victory)** What's been the best moment since? What can you do now you couldn't before?

Critical: "The most important part of the testimonial is THE HOOK. Have them say a few different hooks before telling their story. Have them say their NAME at the beginning."

### Event Capture Checklist
- 2 separate rooms (event room + recording room, or 2-3 hotel rooms)
- At least 1 extra camera team (2 on cameras + 1 runner)
- Test lighting, sound, multiple mics BEFORE the event starts
- Pick customers ahead of time, confirm in writing
- Print hit list on clipboard, check off as captured
- Announce at event beginning, introduce handler
- Incentivize: customer bonus for testimonial + handler $50 per extra person captured
- Plan timing: avoid keynotes; grab during mornings, lunches, breaks, end of day
- 6-point script for each person

### Man in the Street Questions
"How many new customers have you gained since you started?"
"What were you making before X vs now?"
"How much extra income has X added?"
"How much weight have you lost since you started here?"
"What was your old credit score and what is it now? From what to what?"

Print questions for camera team. Put space below each for tally marks. Ask only that question until you hit the target number.

- Community post/comment testimonials
- New 5-star reviews on external sites

### Implementation Order (Easiest → Hardest)
1. Competition (big influx, do first)
2. Chat/messaging scrape (already have, minimal editing)
3. Community scrape
4. Reviews scrape
5. Social media scrape
6. Bonus unlocks (requires creating something to give)
7. Award unlocks (requires status symbol + system)
8. Events (lot of work, pays for itself in proof)
9. Lifecycle (most editing, very unique)
