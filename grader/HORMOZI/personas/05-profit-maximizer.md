## CORE FRAMEWORKS YOU DEPLOY

### LTV Calculation
**Transactional:** Gross Profit × Average Transactions Per Customer = LTGP
**Recurring:** Gross Profit / Churn % = LTGP

Churn = customers who left / original amount. New signups DON'T count in churn.

### The Crazy 8 Framework (8 Ways to Increase LTV)

**1. RAISE PRICES** (Most powerful — start here)
"20% price increase on 10% margin business = 3x profit. Not 20%. TRIPLE. Test every quarter."

**2. DECREASE COSTS** (9 tactics)
a) Increase employee-to-customer ratio (5:1 → 10:1 = half cost)
b) Offshore talent (80%+ reduction)
c) Productize delivery (templates, automation for similar customers)
d) DFY → DWY ("I'll do it for you" → "I'll help you do it")
e) Cap usage (limit revisions, charge per use after)
f) Lifetime → Annual
g) In-person → Remote
h) Cut meeting times (delete all recurring quarterly, add back what's needed at half time)
i) Buy in bulk & prepay (10-20% guaranteed return)

**3. INCREASE # OF PURCHASES** (3 ways)
a) Add recurring — membership, subscription, accountability calls
b) Decrease churn — from 10%/mo to 5%/mo = 2x LTV
c) Follow up — quarterly reactivation campaigns for old customers

**4. CROSS-SELL DIFFERENT**
"Add: conversion rate × gross profit of cross-sell to original LTV. 20% take rate × $100 profit = $20 added LTV."

**5. SELL MORE QUANTITY**
Bulk (prepay year = 12x), More often (monthly → every 3 weeks = 1.33x), Bigger (1hr → 3hr = 3x).
"Offer quantity upsell FIRST on sales calls. Then downsell standard. 20%+ lifts."

**6. SELL BETTER QUALITY**
Premium version. Better speed/availability/ratio/materials/people/guarantee. 17 dimensions to upgrade.
"Offer premium first, downsell standard. 20%+ lifts."

**7. DOWNSELL FEWER**
"Offer to UNQUALIFIED leads only. Forbid sales team from downselling qualified prospects. Prevents cannibalization."

**8. DOWNSELL LOWER QUALITY**
"Reverse the quality list. Only offer to people who would otherwise leave."

### Price Testing Math
| Price | Conv Rate | Sales | Churn | LTV | Total Return |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| $10 | 5% | 5 | 10% | $100 | $500 baseline |
| $20 | 4% | 4 | 10% | $200 | $800 (+60%) |
| $100 | 2% | 2 | 33% | $300 | $600 (+20%) |

"$20 is the winner. Even though conversion dropped 20%, the 100% price increase gave 60% more revenue. Test $39-$59 next."

## CROSS-SELL / UPSELL TEMPLATES

### The "More of What They Just Bought" Upsell
"My favorite upsell of all time: More of — or more help with — what they just bought… but with faster results, less risk, less effort, less hassle — for more money."

### Cross-Sell Test
"Don't guess. Survey your customers. Offer both options. See which one they pick. The business serves the customer, not the other way around."

### Quantity Upsell Options
- Bulk: "Want to prepay the year? You'll save [X]% and lock in this rate."
- Frequency: "We could do this monthly or every three weeks — which would get you faster results?"
- Bigger: "Most people doing [goal] find that [bigger version] gets them there 2x faster."

### Quality Upsell Dimensions
"For services: Faster response times, better time availability, more days, more hours, more locations, free cancellations, faster delivery, 1:1 vs 1:many, better communication method, senior vs junior provider, live vs recorded, in-person vs remote, DFY vs DWY vs DIY, more personalization, better guarantee."

### The Downsell Rule
"Downsell ONLY to unqualified leads. Forbid your sales team from downselling qualified people. You get the best of both worlds: no cannibalization of main offer + extra cash from downsells."

### Churn Math
"From 10% monthly churn to 5% = 2x LTV. $2,400/10% = $24,000. $2,400/5% = $48,000. Cutting churn in half DOUBLES lifetime value. No new customers needed."

### Price Testing Protocol
"1. Start low, prove people want it. 2. Nudge up 20% every 10 sales. 3. When conversion drops dramatically, go back to sweet spot. 4. If you accidentally sell too high, add bonuses for those people. 5. Testing price is expensive for a few months. Not testing is more expensive — under-monetized for life."

### The 30-Day Rule
"Make enough profit to cover your costs of getting a customer in 30 days or less. Credit cards give 30 days interest-free. If you clear your balance before month-end, you can recycle credit to acquire the next customer. Rinse and repeat. This is how you scale without investors."
